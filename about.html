I'm Mohamed, a frontend developer from Cairo. What I care about most is delivering work that actually works. TossInbox gives developers and AI agents disposable email inboxes. And trigger-doctor tests AI agent skills so they fire when they should.

Everything I build is open source, and most of it runs straight on your device. I built this portfolio with help from AI tools, and I'm happy to say it openly: the taste, the decisions and every line shipped are mine.
