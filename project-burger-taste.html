Burger Taste

The story

Problem, approach, outcome — the short version of how this got built.

01The problem

The restaurant needed a recognisable identity that stays clear on compact takeaway materials while carrying both Arabic and English naming.

02The approach

A deep black field and high-contrast golden yellow frame a shield-shaped burger-and-cutlery emblem, with a controlled bilingual wordmark hierarchy for the brand cover and packaging sleeve.

03The outcome

A packaging-ready identity application that gives Burger Taste a clear, recognisable takeaway signature.
