Waslfy

Paste a messy Egyptian WhatsApp order, get clean shipment data — name, phone, governorate, address, and COD amount — printed as an A6 receipt with a Code128 barcode. Runs 100% offline on the merchant’s device.

The story

Problem, approach, outcome — the short version of how this got built.

01The problem

Egyptian merchants receive their orders as free-text WhatsApp messages: typos, dialect, missing fields. Retyping each one into the courier’s form is slow, and one wrong digit means a failed delivery.

02The approach

A parser core of about 13 kB trained against a labeled corpus of real Egyptian order messages, running entirely in the browser as a PWA. Zero network calls, A6 receipt printing with a Code128 barcode, and the parser shipped separately as the waslfy-parser npm package.

03The outcome

100% extraction accuracy on the evaluation corpus, zero network calls at runtime, and a live app any merchant can open from any phone — plus an open-source parser other tools can build on.
