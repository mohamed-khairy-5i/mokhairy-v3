About

The person behind the commits.

I'm Mohamed — a frontend developer from Cairo who cares that things actually ship. React and TypeScript by day; Arabic-first products after hours. Waslfy helps Egyptian COD merchants turn WhatsApp messages into printable shipments. TossInbox gives developers — and their AI agents — throwaway inboxes with no sign-ups. trigger-doctor keeps agent skills honest.

Everything I build is open source, and most of it runs on the edge — or fully on your device. This profile and portfolio are maintained with AI pair-programming, and I'm comfortable being upfront about that: the taste, the decisions and the shipping are mine.
